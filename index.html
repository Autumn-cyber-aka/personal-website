About Me

I am a second-year undergraduate student at Columbia University, where I study Computer Science. I am currently building a foundation in programming, data structures and algorithms, computer systems, and machine learning through coursework and independent projects.

Currently, I am working on reasoning-aware visual representations for vision-language model agents, exploring how models can dynamically re-encode visual inputs as their reasoning evolves and use internal feedback to refine perception.

More broadly, I am interested in multimodal learning, self-improving agents, and continual learning—especially systems that can adapt their knowledge and behavior over time while remaining grounded in the environment.
